## Case-study standard

Each folder distinguishes between:

- **implemented and observed** behaviour;
- **prototype/demo** behaviour;
- **planned or pending verification** work.

No production credentials, personal contact details, trade secrets or unverified impact metrics are published here. Historical screenshots are selected to demonstrate product behavior without exposing detailed personal records.
